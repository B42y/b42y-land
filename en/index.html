GOOD TO KNOW

A few useful answers

Here’s what to expect from the first release.

Does Ba2y work without internet?

Core workflows are designed to work locally without an internet connection. That is a first-release requirement.

Do I need to create an account?

No. The first release is being designed with no mandatory registration, and name-only party creation.

Does it support other currencies?

The first release is focused on the Egyptian pound (EGP), with an Egyptian Arabic-first experience.

Can I send money or take loans with Ba2y?

No. Ba2y is for tracking and recording debts and repayments. It is not a bank, lender, or money transfer service.

What happens if I lose my phone?

Local-only records may be lost with your device. That’s why encrypted backup and restore are part of the first-release plan.

Stop keeping balances in your head.

Ba2y is in development. Follow the project to see when the first release is ready.
